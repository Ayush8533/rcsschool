About Our School

★ क्षेत्र का पहला स्कूल — ऑनलाइन & दूरस्थ माध्यम से शिक्षा  |  गरीब व वंचित छात्रों को शिक्षण सामग्री मुफ़्त

RCS Senior Secondary School traces its origin to the year 2020, when it was formally inaugurated in March 2021 by Sri Mishri Lal — a renowned personality in politics and society. It was established under the aegis of Krishna Welfare Educational Society, Bareilly, with the noble notion of "Learning Without Burden."

Our school is a Senior Secondary, Co-Educational, English Medium school. It is an institution that provides regulare mode as well as distance and open education along with quality all-round education to boys and girls from classes Play Group to XII.

It further strives to develop a sound mind, a robust physique, and strong moral values which enable students to become leaders of society.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To inspire a generation of confident thinkers, compassionate individuals, and future leaders by creating an environment where learning goes beyond classrooms and excellence becomes a way of life.

Mission: At RCS Senior Secondary School, our mission is to nurture young minds with a perfect blend of knowledge, values, and innovation. We strive to create a vibrant learning space where curiosity is encouraged, character is strengthened, and potential is transformed into achievement.

We are committed to empowering students with the skills, confidence, and integrity needed to succeed in a dynamic world and contribute meaningfully to society.
